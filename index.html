WholeTemple

A verse each morning and one tap for the habits that keep you whole.

Free. No ads, no tracking companies, no account needed.

Why it exists

A while ago my life rearranged itself in a way I did not choose. I will not go into it here. What got me through was not a plan. It was a verse in the morning, a glass of water, a walk, a few quiet minutes before the day started, and the grace to miss one and begin again.

WholeTemple is that routine, made into something you can tap. The small things are not small. Start with the water.

— Dan

How it works

🌱
A verse each morning

Chosen for the goals you set. No feed, no noise. Scripture is quoted word for word.

🔥
Streaks that forgive

Prayer, movement, sleep, water, gratitude, one kind act, a few quiet minutes. Miss a day and a grace day covers you.

🔒
Stays on your device

No account needed. Health data never leaves your phone. Back up to the cloud only if you choose to.

Testing on Android before it's public?

Google releases the Android version once a small group has used it daily for two weeks. Three steps, in this order:

  1. Join the Google Group with the Google account signed in on your phone.
  2. Open the tester page, sign in with the same account, tap Become a tester. If it says the app is not available, step 1 did not finish.
  3. Install and open it once a day. That is the whole job. After a few days, send one thing that annoyed or confused you.
