# WholeTemple

A verse each morning and one tap for the habits that keep you whole:
prayer, movement, sleep, water, gratitude, one kind act, a few quiet minutes.
Free. No ads, no tracking companies.

## iPhone

**[Get it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/wholetemple/id6753139244)**

## Android

The Android version is in closed testing. Google will not release it until a
small group of people has used it daily for two weeks. Joining takes two
minutes and three steps, **in this order**:

1. **Join the tester group.**
   **[Join the Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/wholetemple-testers)**
   Use the Google account that is signed in on your Android phone.

2. **Become a tester.**
   **[Open the tester page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.wholetemple.app)**
   Sign in with the **same Google account** and tap **Become a tester**.
   If it says the app is not available, step 1 did not finish. Go back and join the group first.

3. **Install and open it once a day.**
   The tester page links to the Play Store listing. Install, then open the app each morning for two weeks. That is the whole job.
